About Me

Designing experiences
that actually work

I'm Srimathi, a UI/UX Designer at Lucid IT Solution with 2 years of hands-on experience designing hospitality and restaurant management products — including POS systems, CRM platforms, online ordering systems, and operational dashboards.

I hold a B.Tech in Computer Science Engineering, which gives me a strong technical foundation to collaborate effectively with developers and translate complex workflows into clean, intuitive interfaces.

My design philosophy centres on operational efficiency and user clarity — making sure that the people who use these products daily can do their jobs faster, with less friction and fewer errors.
